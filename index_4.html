Watchdog Agent

Good flats in Barcelona go in hours, and so do good jobs. I was tired of refreshing, so I built a bot that does the watching for me.

Two bots, one engine. BuscPiso sweeps Barcelona agencies, Habitaclia, Pisos.com, Idealista and Fotocasa every 2 hours, filters out expired and seasonal lets, remembers what it's already seen and posts only new matches to a Telegram group. HireMe scans job boards, has an LLM score each role against my profile out of 5, and if I reply with a number, it writes a tailored CV and cover letter.

Same idea as IT alert triage: lots of noise in, a few ranked, useful signals out.

03About

The longer version.

assets/me-2.jpg

I studied neuroscience because I wanted to know why people do what they do. I still do. It's the lens I bring to everything I build: tech should fit how people actually behave, not how we wish they did.

My path hasn't been a straight line. I taught logic and neuroscience at master's level, learnt front-end development on the job at e-commerce startups, then spent a few years in high-ticket sales and account management, including on the Airbnb business at Teleperformance. Sales taught me the most useful thing a builder can know: what people will actually pay for.

"Get 1% better every day. It adds up faster than you think."

Now I'm doing an MSc in Machine Learning & AI (GenAI track) and building in public in Barcelona. Outside of code you'll find me moving every day, reading every day, and probably automating something that annoys me.
