Problem: Good listings (flats, jobs) are gone within hours, and filtering them by hand is noise.

What it does: It monitors sources, filters and ranks each new item with an LLM against my criteria, then sends only what matters to Telegram or iMessage.

Why it matters: It's the same pattern as alert triage for IT teams: lots of signals in, a few ranked actions out.
